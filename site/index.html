Spudnyk
AI Assistant

A personal Telegram assistant that helps its owner capture tasks, manage reminders and synchronize explicitly requested events with Google Calendar.

Owner controlled

Access is restricted to the authorized owner. External actions require clear intent and appropriate confirmation.

Useful every day

Tasks, notes, reminders, daily planning and weekly review are combined in one operating loop.

Minimal access

Google Calendar access is used only to provide the calendar functionality requested by the owner.

Privacy Policy

This is the public information page for the OAuth-enabled personal assistant. It does not provide access to the assistant or its private data.
