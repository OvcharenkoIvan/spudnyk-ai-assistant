Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Spudnyk AI Assistant is currently a private, single-owner personal assistant. This public policy explains its use of Google user data.

Data processed

The assistant processes information intentionally provided by its authorized owner, including Telegram messages, tasks, notes, reminders, and Google Calendar event data required for calendar synchronization.

How Google data is used

Google Calendar data is used only to display, create, update, delete and synchronize calendar events explicitly requested by the authorized owner. Google user data is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Storage and protection

Application data is stored in a private production environment. OAuth credentials are encrypted at rest. Access to the assistant is owner-restricted, and operational logs are designed not to expose credentials or private event contents.

Service providers

The assistant relies on service providers necessary to operate its functionality, including Google Calendar, Telegram, OpenAI and infrastructure hosting. Each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy commitments. Access is limited to what is needed for the requested functionality.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained only while needed for the assistant's operation and recovery requirements. The owner can delete tasks, notes and events, revoke Google access in Google Account security settings, and request deletion of stored application data.

Sharing

Personal data is not sold. It is not disclosed to unrelated third parties, except where required by law or necessary for the service providers listed above to deliver the requested functionality.

Contact

Privacy or deletion requests may be sent to the developer contact address displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.

Changes

This policy will be updated before the assistant is offered to additional users or commercial customers. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date.
